# Product, customer and growth batch — 2026-10-15

The source register is `../../sources/product-growth.json`; startup sources remain separately resolvable. This batch adds cohort analysis, RICE, North Star and Value Proposition Canvas plus three proposed diagnostic graphs. It does not claim a complete market or product science library.

RICE is a creator-described prioritization heuristic. Multiplying ordinal impact estimates does not turn them into measured utility. Its confidence input is not a calibrated probability of success and must not populate the general evidence-confidence field. Dependencies, mandatory work and strategic constraints may override a score with a recorded reason.

North Star is a strategy-alignment heuristic. A candidate input-to-outcome relationship is a hypothesis, not automatic causality. A single metric can be gamed or hide customer harm, unit economics and multi-sided effects. Contextual guardrails and unit-level measures remain necessary.

Cohort definitions require explicit entity, entry, return event, interval, eligibility and observation windows. User, account and revenue retention are different. Return-on, return-on-or-after, and continuous survival definitions cannot be interchanged. Recent cohorts may not yet be observable at later intervals. A post-entry behavioral segment can select survivors; associations between activation and retention do not prove a causal intervention.

The reviewed Amplitude calculation and interpretation pages contain simplified prose and examples that can be read ambiguously about on-or-after versus by-time retention. This knowledge uses explicit return intervals and eligibility rather than copying a verbal shortcut or vendor screenshot. No illustrative vendor percentages become a benchmark.

The Value Proposition Canvas narrows business-model discussion to customer needs and offer value. It complements BMC and JTBD; it neither measures market attractiveness nor validates pricing by itself. No copyrighted template graphic is redistributed.

Diagnostic graphs are Startino product syntheses informed by measurement documentation and practitioner sources. They enumerate plausible explanations and discriminating evidence, rather than assigning a root cause from a chart. Multiple branches can apply. Unknown evidence routes to collection; it is not equivalent to a negative test. Payment failure and voluntary cancellation are separated; no Stripe-specific state rule is assumed for another billing system.

Deferred: independent causal/longitudinal validation, full product strategy and discovery methods, segmentation/ICP validation, TAM/SAM/SOM methodology, marketplace liquidity, pricing research, sales-led activation, RICE alternatives, and sector-specific thresholds. No full paywalled book, subscription playbook or embedded video was accessed.
