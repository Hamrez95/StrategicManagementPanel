# Startup research batch — 2026-10-15

This is knowledge content only. The six-phase journey and all routing predicates are **Startino product syntheses**, not a published or validated universal maturity scale. Source IDs resolve in `../../sources/startup.json`. Records preserve uncertainty rather than promising startup success.

## Reconciliation

- Blank defines startup work by business-model search. Ries emphasizes a learning loop. Aulet provides a disciplined set of questions and steps; YC emphasizes direct founder learning and speed. They inform a dependency graph, but none establishes these exact six phases.
- An MVP is an experiment artifact; problem–solution fit and PMF are evidence assessments. They are not mandatory separate company stages. A business may run multiple experiments and business models simultaneously.
- YC's vivid demand-based description of PMF is qualitative and oriented toward venture startups. Here, PMF is a segment-specific hypothesis reviewed with repeated behavior, customer value and economics. No universal numeric PMF score, funding-round mapping or employee threshold is asserted.
- BMC records assumptions; a filled canvas is not proof. JTBD enriches understanding of motivation; it does not substitute for market sizing, payment behavior or causal experiments.
- The Aulet excerpt includes an LTV-to-CAC heuristic. It is not adopted as a universal gate: definitions, discounting, margins, funding constraints, customer lifetime and acquisition dynamics vary. The journey uses observed economics and an explicit local decision criterion instead.
- Stage (commercial uncertainty), size (resources/complexity), maturity (capabilities), performance trend (stable/growing/declining) and intervention (turnaround/transformation) are orthogonal. Mature organizations can create idea-stage ventures. Small firms can be operationally mature.

## Evidence design

Persist the segment, geography, business model, time window, cohort definition, source, collection method, sample limitations, contradictions and review date with each claim. Interview statements inform hypotheses; actual usage, payment and renewal can provide different evidence. A signed letter of intent can still be non-binding. Averages and aggregate growth may conceal cohort deterioration.

Question gates, completion fields, evidence-status enums and routing are proposed product contracts. They require user research and expert review before automation. Unknown is distinct from false. An evidence conflict requests review; it does not silently overwrite an older claim. No percentage confidence is invented from checklist completion.

## Context exceptions

Hardware may need feasibility and capital tests before deployment; regulated and safety-critical businesses need permitted prototypes and domain approval. Enterprise products may have long procurement and renewal windows; repeat usage alone may be inappropriate. Marketplaces require side-specific liquidity, supply quality and contribution economics. One-off purchases require repeatable acquisition, delivery and customer outcomes rather than compulsory frequent repeat use. Nonprofits need beneficiary outcomes and payer/funder economics separately.
