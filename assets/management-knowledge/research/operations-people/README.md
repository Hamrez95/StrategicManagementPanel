# Operations and organization research — 2026-10-15

Delivered subset: six frameworks (Lean Thinking, TOC, DMAIC, Kotter change, McKinsey 7S and team psychological safety), eight source records and three editorial diagnosis trees (inventory, delivery and strategy execution).

## What the sources support

- LEI's [Lean Thinking and Practice](https://www.lean.org/lexicon-terms/lean-thinking-and-practice/) supplies five principles, while [Lean Operations](https://www.lean.org/explore-lean/operations/) distinguishes waste from essential nonvalue-added work and describes flow/pull. Neither is used to calculate inventory targets.
- [Goldratt Research Labs](https://www.goldrattresearchlabs.com/introduction-to-toc) supplies the five focusing steps and the whole-system focus. The pack does not assert that every observed queue proves a unique, stable constraint.
- [ASQ DMAIC](https://asq.org/quality-resources/dmaic) supplies the five phases and the boundary of improving an existing process. This is one Six Sigma workflow, not full coverage of statistical techniques, certifications or process sigma conversion.
- [Kotter](https://www.kotterinc.com/methodology/8-steps/) supplies current accelerator names and evolution from the initial linear model. Commercial claims of a universally proven method are not adopted as independent causal evidence.
- [McKinsey](https://www.mckinsey.com/capabilities/people-and-organization/our-insights/enduring-ideas-the-7-s-framework) supports coordination and interdependence. Its accessible article did not expose all seven labels; the [ILO institutional guide, printed page 28](https://www.ilo.org/sites/default/files/wcmsp5/groups/public/@ed_dialogue/@act_emp/documents/publication/wcms_595731.pdf) supplements the names. The ILO guide is explicitly secondary to the model originators, not mislabelled original research.
- [Edmondson's 2002 HBS paper](https://www.hbs.edu/ris/Publication%20Files/02-062_0b5726a8-443d-4629-9e75-736679b870fc.pdf) supports the interpersonal-risk construct, distinction from trust/cohesion and learning through action/reflection. The original 1999 HBS item page returned 403; this pack does not claim to have read that article directly. Observational studies discussed by the author are not treated as randomized proof of each intervention suggested here.

## Derived content and limits

All framework applicability tags, decision gates, proposed evidence fields, examples and compatibility links are editorial. Source-supported model dimensions are separated in each record. No inherent incompatibility was identified among these six frameworks; empty conflict lists are not evidence that every implementation choice is compatible. Lean inventory reduction and TOC protective buffers, for example, require shared goal and variability assumptions rather than blanket adoption of minimum inventory.

All three diagnosis trees are analyst-derived candidate inquiry paths. Their branching order is a convenience for investigation, not a probability ranking, validated diagnosis, exhaustive cause taxonomy or quoted source-authored tree. Each path has unknown-data handling, evidence requests and follow-up work. Multiple causes may coexist. The trees cannot establish causality from delay, silence, queues, correlations or survey answers alone. They avoid clinical diagnosis, employee scoring and automatic staff or inventory cuts.
