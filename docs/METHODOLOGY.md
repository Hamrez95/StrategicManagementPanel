# Methodology and reference boundaries

This prototype translates selected strategic-management frameworks into a guided Persian workspace. It does not claim to cover every MBA/DBA framework or replace evidence-based managerial judgment. All company data and decisions are fictional.

## Calculation conventions

| Tool | Convention in this prototype | Boundary |
| --- | --- | --- |
| EFE / IFE | Each group's weights sum to 100%; rating 1–4; weighted total 1–4 | EFE rates response to the environment; IFE rates internal weakness/strength. They are not interchangeable meanings. |
| QSPM | Both weight groups independently total 1; AS 1–4; TAS = weight × AS | A factor marked irrelevant is excluded across all three strategies. Scores compare relative attractiveness, not likelihood of success. |
| SPACE | FS/IS positive 1–6; CA/ES negative −6 to −1; X = IS + CA, Y = FS + ES | Workshop judgments and axis convention must remain consistent. |
| IE | EFE vertical and IFE horizontal; thresholds 2 and 3 | Grow/build, hold/maintain and harvest/divest are starting points, not automatic decisions. |
| BCG | Descending logarithmic relative-share axis; market-growth cutoff editable; bubble size related to revenue | Review profitability, synergies and market definitions separately. |
| GE | Aggregated workshop scores 1–5; low/medium/high bins | Educational simplification, not a full weighted factor-level GE model. |
| Five forces / VRIO / TOWS | Structured qualitative judgment with supporting notes | A radar or score does not create an authoritative universal industry-attractiveness index. |
| BSC / execution | Objective, measure, baseline, target, owner and linked initiative | Progress supports decreasing targets; a strategy-map link is a hypothesis, not proven causality. |
| Risk | Probability 1–5 × impact 1–5 | Ordinal internal prioritization, not an expected monetary loss calculation. |
| ROA | Net income / average beginning and ending assets | The displayed example is monthly, not silently annualized. |
| ROE | Net income / equity | Prototype uses supplied period equity, not an undisclosed average. |
| CAC / LTV | Acquisition spending / new customers; LTV uses order value, frequency, lifetime and contribution margin | Cohort retention and attribution are simplified; no universal desirable threshold asserted. |
| Runway | Cash / negative monthly net cash flow | Returns “not applicable” when cash flow is nonnegative. |
| Break-even | Fixed costs / contribution-margin ratio | Undefined when contribution margin is nonpositive. |
| NPV / IRR | Consistent per-period flows and discount rate; IRR only for conventional flows | No misleading single IRR for multiple sign changes. |
| Scenario | Volume growth at constant selling price; variable unit-cost inflation; fixed costs held constant | Sensitivity calculation is distinct from narrative strategic scenarios and probabilities. |

## Primary sources consulted

- [David, David and David — QSPM research (2016)](https://www.strategyclub.com/wp-content/uploads/2016/04/Journal-of-Strategic-Marketing-1.pdf): factor weighting and explicit attractiveness comparisons. This prototype uses the textbook 1–4 AS convention, not every alternative ranking convention in the literature.
- [Porter — Harvard Business School, The Five Forces](https://www.isc.hbs.edu/strategy/business-strategy/Pages/the-five-forces.aspx): industry structure and competitive pressures.
- [Boston Consulting Group — Growth-share matrix](https://www.bcg.com/about/overview/our-history/growth-share-matrix): portfolio analysis.
- [Balanced Scorecard Institute — BSC basics](https://balancedscorecard.org/bsc-basics-overview/): connecting strategic objectives, measures and initiatives.
- [Oregon State University — Strategy Analysis Framework](https://open.oregonstate.education/strategicmanagement/chapter/8-strategy-analysis-framework-saf/): strategy-analysis tools and framework organization.
- [OpenStax — Principles of Finance](https://openstax.org/details/books/principles-finance): financial interpretation and investment-analysis background.
- [McKinsey — How strategy champions win (2025)](https://www.mckinsey.com/capabilities/strategy-and-corporate-finance/our-insights/how-strategy-champions-win): execution and mobilization perspective.
- [McKinsey — The art, science, and technology of geopolitical scenario planning (2026)](https://www.mckinsey.com/capabilities/geopolitics/our-insights/the-art-science-and-technology-of-geopolitical-scenario-planning): uncertainty, scenarios and watchpoints; not a validation of the simplified financial simulator.
- [Buffer — refreshed navigation and visual design (2026)](https://buffer.com/changelog/a-refreshed-navigation-visual-design-for-buffer): design inspiration only.

References support specific concepts, not a blanket certification of the product. Educational help includes the corresponding sources and explicit cautions. No proprietary textbook prose was reproduced.
